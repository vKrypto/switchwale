# SwitchWala — Feature Guide

Every feature, ordered by how much it matters to a job seeker actually
using this. Tier 1 is why you'd sign up; Tier 3 is what keeps you here.

---

## Tier 1 — Core

### 1. Multi-Channel Auto-Apply

**What it is:** Apply to jobs automatically on Naukri, LinkedIn, Instahyre,
and JSearch — no manual "Apply" click required.

**Why it matters:** The single biggest time sink in a job search is the
apply click itself, repeated hundreds of times. This removes it entirely.

**How it works:**
- Every job detail page has a one-click **Auto Apply** button
- Every saved search / job alert has its own **Auto apply** on/off toggle,
  so new matching postings get applied to the moment they're found
- Runs on a schedule in the background — new jobs get applied to while
  you sleep

![Auto Apply on a job detail page](../sales/images/job-detail-auto-apply.png)

---

### 2. AI-Tailored Resume + ATS Score

**What it is:** Paste a job description, the AI rewrites your resume
sections — summary, experience bullets, skills — to match it, and scores
the result against that JD.

**Why it matters:** A generic resume gets filtered by ATS software before
a human ever sees it. A resume tuned to the exact JD keywords gets through.
2,200+ tailored resumes generated to date.

**How it works:**
- Every applied job gets its own resume PDF and ATS match score (0–100)
- Scores and PDFs are all listed and searchable in one table by company,
  role, platform, and skill

![Per-job resumes with ATS score](../sales/images/job-resumes-ats-score.png)

---

### 3. AI Cover Letter Generator

**What it is:** Paste the same JD, get a tailored cover letter written as
Markdown and rendered straight to a polished PDF — no template needed.

**Why it matters:** Cover letters are the thing everyone skips because
they take too long to write well. This makes them free.

![Cover letter generator](../sales/images/resume-maker-cover-letter.png)

---

### 4. Unified Application Tracking Dashboard

**What it is:** Every application, every status — applied, shortlisted,
recommended, discovered, rejected, expired — across every connected
platform, in one dashboard.

**Why it matters:** Nobody can track hundreds of applications across five
different sites in their head or a spreadsheet. This does it automatically.

![Dashboard overview](../sales/images/dashboard-overview.png)

---

## Tier 2 — High-Value Differentiators

### 5. Universal AI Assistant ("AI Reply")

**What it is:** A floating AI Reply button on every page. Click it on any
recruiter message — Gmail, LinkedIn, or Instahyre — and it drafts a
human-sounding reply in your voice, ready to send or edit.

**Why it matters:** Recruiter conversations die from slow replies. This
makes replying take seconds instead of minutes, on any channel.

![AI Reply on an Instahyre conversation](../sales/images/instahyre-conversations-ai-reply.png)

---

### 6. Auto-Referral Outreach

**What it is:** Sends referral requests to recruiters and hiring managers
by email and LinkedIn DM, written in your voice, at scale — with reply
tracking and one-click "ask for follow-up".

**Why it matters:** A referral is the highest-leverage way into a company,
but writing and tracking hundreds of personalized asks by hand is
impossible. 1,000+ sent and tracked so far.

![Referral email sent](../sales/images/referral-email-sent.png)
![LinkedIn referral message and reply thread](../sales/images/referral-linkedin-message.png)

---

### 7. Calendar Auto-Sync

**What it is:** Interview invites are parsed straight out of your inbox
and dropped onto your calendar automatically — no manual entry, ever.

**Why it matters:** Missing an interview because it got buried in an
inbox is the worst possible failure mode. This makes it impossible.

![Calendar auto-updated with a confirmed interview](../sales/images/calendar-auto-synced-interview.png)

---

### 8. One-Click Actions Inbox

**What it is:** Every recruiter reply, LinkedIn connection request, and
"apply now" recommendation lands in one prioritized list with a
Snooze / Done / Dismiss button — plus a "Bulk auto-apply" action for
batches of new matches.

**Why it matters:** You don't have to remember to check five different
apps. One inbox, one click per item, done.

![One-click Actions inbox](../sales/images/actions-inbox-one-click.png)

---

### 9. Multi-Channel Connectors

**What it is:** Gmail, Google Calendar, LinkedIn, Naukri, Instahyre, and
JSearch all connected and synced live from one screen. WhatsApp and Call
are wired in as "coming soon".

**Why it matters:** This is the plumbing that makes everything else
possible — one login, every channel, always in sync.

![Connectors overview](../sales/images/connectors-overview.png)

---

### 10. Conversations Hub

**What it is:** Recruiter messages from Gmail, LinkedIn, and Instahyre
normalized into threaded, searchable inboxes — with AI-compose and
AI-reply built in.

**Why it matters:** Recruiter outreach doesn't stay on one platform.
Neither should your inbox.

![Gmail mails with AI-compose](../sales/images/gmail-mails-ai-compose.png)

---

## Tier 3 — Supporting Tools

### 11. Naukri Profile Optimizer

**What it is:** Your Naukri profile broken into sections — headline, key
skills, summary, and more — each with a current score, an AI-optimized
rewrite, and one click to push the fix back to naukri.com.

**Why it matters:** Recruiters search Naukri by keyword before they ever
see your resume. A weak profile means you never show up in that search.

![Profile optimizer with per-section scores](../sales/images/profile-optimizer-resume-on-file.png)

---

### 12. Job Board & Discovery

**What it is:** Every job seen across every connected source, browsable
and filterable by company, location, salary, skills, channel, and status,
with favorites.

**Why it matters:** One board instead of five open tabs to five job sites.

![Job board with discovery filters](../sales/images/job-board-discovery.png)

---

### 13. Companies & Contacts Directory

**What it is:** Every company and every point-of-contact seen across all
channels, with bulk email-fill, email verification, and duplicate
removal.

**Why it matters:** Referral outreach and recruiter tracking both run on
having clean, deduplicated contact data — this is where it lives.

![Contacts directory](../sales/images/contacts-directory.png)
![Companies directory](../sales/images/companies-directory.png)

---

### 14. Job Alerts & Saved Searches

**What it is:** Naukri search-URL watchers that auto-import new postings
and refetch job descriptions on a schedule, each with its own
auto-apply toggle.

**Why it matters:** Set the search criteria once, never manually re-search
again.

![Job alerts with per-search auto-apply toggle](../sales/images/job-alerts-auto-apply-toggle.png)

---

### 15. Hot Keywords & Market Intelligence

**What it is:** The skills and keywords trending across all scanned job
postings, plus the exact keywords recruiters used to search for and find
your profile.

**Why it matters:** Tells you what to add to your resume and profile
before you apply, not after you get rejected.

![Hot keywords and recruiter search terms](../sales/images/hot-keywords-market-intel.png)

---

### 16. Activity & Audit Log

**What it is:** A full history of every background automation run —
task lifecycle, connector syncs, failures — plus a notifications feed.

**Why it matters:** When something goes wrong (a sync fails, an apply
doesn't go through), you can see exactly what happened and when.

![Audit log of every automation run](../sales/images/activity-audit-log.png)

---

### 17. Settings & Preferences

**What it is:** Profile details, resume attachments, application
preferences, interview availability windows, proxy configuration, and
choice of LLM provider (OpenAI / Anthropic) — all in one place.

**Why it matters:** The automation only works well if it knows your
constraints. This is where you set them, once.

![Interview availability settings](../sales/images/settings-interview-availability.png)

---

**Internal / admin tooling** (user management, global job scheduler
control, LLM cost analytics) exists to run the platform but is not
customer-facing — intentionally left out of this guide.
